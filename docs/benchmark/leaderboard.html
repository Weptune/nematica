NEMATICA worm benchmark: reference copies

A level passes when it is inside in at least 2 of a split's 3 held-out pools (two thirds when a split has another number of pools). At rest: at least 5 of the 6 activity levels pass. When poked: echo pattern, echo size and echo spread all pass. Each level's cell shows the copy's median excess distance (0 = as different from real worms as real worms are from each other), averaged over splits; its colour shows how often the level passes: green always, red never, grey not judged. Levels are on different scales, so compare down a column, not across a row.
