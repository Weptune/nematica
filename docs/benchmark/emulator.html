What each level measures

activity distributionhow each neuron class's activity values are distributed
timescalehow long each class's activity takes to forget itself (autocorrelation half-life)
correlation patternwhich classes rise and fall together
dimensionalityhow many independent patterns the activity spreads over
lead and lagwhich classes tend to lead and which to follow, at about 2 and 6 s
reversal-bout timinghow long the reversal command neuron AVA stays high
echo patternwhich classes answer a poke of which, and with what sign
echo sizehow strongly neurons answer pokes
echo spreadhow much of the brain is significantly moved by a single poke
echo time coursethe time course of the strongest answers
echo complexityhow compressible the pattern of significant answers is

Every level is a distance between two worms, computed the same way for real and simulated worms, on neuron classes both have.

How to read this

  • Inside is not agreement. It means no difference from real worm-to-worm variation was detected in that pool. With a few held-out pools of a few dozen worms, small but consistent differences can pass. Look at the dots: a level whose dots all sit on one side of zero is consistently off, even when it passes.
  • Too typical is a failure too: the copy's worms are closer to real worms than real worms are to each other, as an average worm would be. Real worms differ from each other, and a good copy should too.
  • Lead and lag has little power: real worms agree with each other on it only weakly (correlation about 0.03), so most copies sit near zero.
  • Echo time course and echo complexity are reported but are not part of the rule. Echo complexity mostly measures ongoing activity, not the poke (README, sham checks), and fails for every model tried.
  • The splits rearrange the same worms; noise seeds resample only the simulation, not the fit. Passing several splits under several seeds shows robustness, not confirmation on new animals.
