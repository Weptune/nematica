# Strategy after Studies 2 and 3 (17 Nov 2026)

The full note is a shared doc ("NEMATICA strategy after Studies 2 and 3"). This file keeps its decisions in
the repository.

- **Where we stand:**
  - No single property separates real from artificial networks in kind.
  - Study 2 tested the slow-internal-state thesis directly and it did not hold.
  - Study 3 stopped at calibration twice.
- **Recurring limits:**
  - too few repeats per animal;
  - the recording instrument dominates;
  - uncontrolled input drive (a movie's changing envelope);
  - leads that shrink to known facts.
- **Recommendation:**
  - **A**, a many-repeat study on the Allen OpenScope Barcoding dataset (DANDI 000563: 14 mice, six
    Neuropixels probes, an 8 s white-noise flicker repeated about 90 times in the example session), gated
    in this order:
    1. metadata feasibility; stop if fewer than 10 mice have at least 50 repeats;
    2. calibration before any claim;
    3. one or two claims;
    4. review, freeze, run.
  - **C** alongside: a short preprint draft from the worm benchmark and the instrument lesson, shown to
    the user before anything is shared.
- **Process changes:**
  - calibrate before drafting claims;
  - check repeats and drive first;
  - run one novelty search per lead;
  - one headline claim per study;
  - commit working scripts as they run.
- **Status:** awaiting the user's go-ahead on A and C.
