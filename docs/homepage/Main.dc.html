A TURING TEST FOR ARTIFICIAL BRAINS

Real worms are weird.
Fake ones are average.

People are building digital nervous systems. Nobody agrees how to tell if one is right. NEMATICA scores artificial brains against whole-brain recordings of real C. elegans, measured in the only unit that matters: how different real worms are from each other.

THE RULER

A model passes when it is as different from real worms as real worms are from each other.

No closer, no further. Too far means wrong. Too close means it learned the average worm, which no real worm is.

TOO TYPICAL
