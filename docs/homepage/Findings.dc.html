WHAT WE FOUND

Looking like a brain and working like one are different things.

Everything below was tested on held-out worms the models never saw, and every prediction was written down before the experiment ran. Results that went against us are kept.

WATCHING
Artificial brains pass every test of ongoing activity, including consciousness-science measures.
POKING

BUILDING

1 · WATCHING CANNOT CERTIFY A COPY

How real a brain looks at rest says nothing about how it answers a poke.

Each dot is one artificial brain scored on one group of held-out worms: how far its ongoing activity is from real worms (left is more real), against how wrong its echoes are when one neuron is switched on (higher is more real). 88 brain scores, rank correlation about zero (-0.06).

A model of a worm's own resting activity predicts that same worm's echoes no better than other worms' models do. Yet each worm's echoes are repeatable (36 of 43 worms): brains have causal fingerprints that watching does not reveal.

3 · SIGNATURES OF A MIND

In a real brain, most of the brain stays out of each echo.

Consciousness research judges unresponsive patients by the complexity of the brain's echo to a magnetic pulse (the perturbational complexity index). Applied to worms, it is the one property no artificial brain matches yet. The reason is simple to state: after a poke, a third of a real brain's neuron types never respond, and the ones that do keep responding; in every model, almost every neuron type flickers briefly.

30 s after a pokeHow long a response lastsNeuron types that respond at all
Real worms1.5 s (median), 3.3 s (mean)66%
Brain learned from rest1.0 s, 2.6 s93%
Brain that passes the poke test0.5 s, 1.7 s95%

At rest, the same artificial brains match real worms on all three consciousness-science measures of ongoing activity (complexity, integration, repertoire of whole-brain states), on two datasets. Nothing here claims a worm or a model is conscious; these are the measurable properties that, in people, separate a responsive brain from an unresponsive one.
